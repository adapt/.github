**Most agents work for one person. Adapt's work for your whole company.** Adapt is the open platform for building company agents.

[Website][adapt] · [Docs][docs] · [Workflows][workflows] · [Changelog][changelog] · [Careers][careers]

![Adapt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adapt/.github/main/profile/images/ascii.webp)

Use the whole platform or any part of it.

**Company agents**

- **[Company agent][product]**: one agent for every team, working in their tools with your company's knowledge.
- **[AI Transformation Services][services]**: our team maps your agent roadmap, builds and runs your agents, and trains your people.

**[Agent infrastructure][primitives]**

- **[Sandboxes][sandboxes]**: an isolated computer for every agent run, kept outside your app.
- **[Storage][storage]**: files and memory that outlast a run, so the next one can build on it.
- **[Model gateway][gateway]**: every major model through one API, switched in configuration rather than code.

The company agent goes open source on January 4, 2027. Until then, [join the waitlist][waitlist].

Made in San Francisco, SOC 2 Type II. See how Adapt [compares with other agent platforms][compare], or browse ready-made skills in [adapt/workflows](https://github.com/adapt/workflows).
